# Calm Companion parent beta invitation

Copy and personalise this invitation before sending it individually.

## Invitation text

Hello,

APC is testing Calm Companion, a free web app that offers one small parent action and simple visual tools for ordinary, non-emergency moments.

Would you be willing to try a short 15-minute usability session? We are testing whether the app is clear and easy to use. We are not testing your parenting or collecting information about your child.

Please do not share names, diagnoses, schools, contact details, histories or other private information during the session or in the feedback form. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.

Calm Companion is general educational parent support. It is not therapy, diagnosis, assessment, medical advice or emergency support. For immediate danger or a medical emergency in Malaysia, call 999.

If you are interested, I will arrange a suitable time and share the controlled testing link.

Thank you.

## Recruitment boundaries

- Invite adult parents or caregivers only.
- Do not recruit anyone currently seeking emergency or crisis support.
- Participation must not affect access to APC services.
- Do not promise that the app will calm a child or improve an outcome.
- Do not collect a child's name, diagnosis, school, medical history, behaviour record or identifying details.
