# Calm Companion controlled-beta invitation

Use this message to recruit five voluntary adult parents or caregivers. Send it privately by WhatsApp, email or direct message. Do not post the controlled-beta link publicly.

## Ready-to-send message

Hello. I am looking for five adult parents or caregivers to help test APC Calm Companion, a free web app with simple parent-support tools for everyday difficult moments.

The session takes about 20 minutes and can be done on your own phone or computer. I will ask you to try several app tasks and tell me what is clear or confusing. We are testing the app, not you, and you can stop at any time.

Please do not share a child’s name, diagnosis, school, contact details, history or other private information. This is general educational parent support, not therapy, diagnosis, assessment, medical advice or emergency support. Participation is voluntary and will not affect access to APC services.

If there is immediate danger or a medical emergency in Malaysia, call 999 instead of using the app or joining this test.

If you would like to take part, please reply with the device you can use:

- iPhone with Safari
- Android phone with Chrome
- desktop or laptop browser

Please do not send any child information in your reply. I will arrange a short test session and provide the private test link then.

## Before sending the test link

Confirm all four points with the participant:

1. They are an adult parent or caregiver participating voluntarily.
2. They are not seeking help for an emergency or immediate safety concern.
3. They agree not to enter identifying child or family information.
4. They understand this is a usability test of general educational support, not evidence of a clinical outcome.

Assign only the next code from P1 to P5. Do not put names or contact details in the launch workbook.

## Coverage target

Recruit at least two iPhone Safari testers, at least two Android Chrome testers, and one desktop or laptop tester. If reasonably available, include one person who already uses larger text, keyboard navigation or a screen reader.

After the participant confirms, follow `parent-beta-facilitator-script.md`. Share only the stable beta URL shown there.
